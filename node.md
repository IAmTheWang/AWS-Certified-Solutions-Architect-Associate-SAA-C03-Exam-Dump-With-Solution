## How long does it take to prepare for each AWS certification?

The time depends on your AWS experience and how many hours you study each week. Assuming you already have software development experience (like a front-end developer), here is a realistic estimate:

| Certification | Full Name                                        | Difficulty | Typical Study Time             |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ---------- | ------------------------------ |
| **SAA**       | AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate    | ⭐⭐☆☆☆      | **1–2 months (60–100 hours)**  |
| **DVA**       | AWS Certified Developer – Associate              | ⭐⭐☆☆☆      | **3–6 weeks (40–80 hours)**    |
| **SOA**       | AWS Certified SysOps Administrator – Associate   | ⭐⭐⭐☆☆      | **2–3 months (80–120 hours)**  |
| **SAP**       | AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Professional | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐      | **3–6 months (150–300 hours)** |

### SAA (Solutions Architect – Associate)

* Best starting point for most people.
* Covers core AWS services such as EC2, S3, VPC, IAM, RDS, Lambda, CloudFront, and Route 53.
* Recommended study time: **1–2 months**.

### DVA (Developer – Associate)

* Focuses on developing applications on AWS.
* Covers Lambda, API Gateway, DynamoDB, SQS, SNS, EventBridge, CodeBuild, and CodePipeline.
* Since much of the content overlaps with SAA, many people prepare in **3–6 weeks** after passing SAA.

### SOA (SysOps Administrator – Associate)

* Focuses on AWS operations and system administration.
* Covers CloudWatch, CloudTrail, Auto Scaling, Systems Manager, monitoring, backup, and troubleshooting.
* Generally considered the most challenging Associate-level certification.
* Recommended study time: **2–3 months**.

### SAP (Solutions Architect – Professional)

* The most advanced certification in this path.
* Covers enterprise architecture, multi-account environments, AWS Organizations, Direct Connect, Transit Gateway, disaster recovery, security, migration, and cost optimization.
* The exam consists of long, scenario-based questions requiring architectural decision-making.
* Recommended study time: **3–6 months**, especially if you already have SAA-level knowledge and some hands-on AWS experience.

## Recommended learning order

1. **SAA**
2. **DVA**
3. **SOA**
4. **SAP**

This order builds your knowledge progressively:

* **SAA** gives you the AWS foundation.
* **DVA** strengthens your cloud development skills.
* **SOA** adds operations and monitoring knowledge.
* **SAP** combines everything at an enterprise architecture level.

If you study **about 2 hours per day, 5 days a week**, you can realistically complete all four certifications in **7–9 months**. With **3–4 hours of study per day**, many candidates finish in **5–6 months**, assuming they stay consistent.
